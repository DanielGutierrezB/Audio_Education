Processing Pipeline

1

Analysis

FFprobe detects format, sample rate, bit depth, channels, and duration. Results inform every subsequent step automatically.

2

Pre-process

Stereo→Mono: Mixes channels to mono (voice is always mono). Denoise (afftdn): Frequency-domain noise reduction, noise floor −25 dB. Quality mode only. Noise Gate: Closes signal during silent passages to eliminate background hum. Silence Trim: Removes leading and trailing silence.

3

EQ Measure

Applies the full EQ and dynamics chain in measurement mode to gather loudness statistics for the two-pass loudnorm algorithm. HPF removes rumble. Presence boost at 2.5 kHz, mud cut at 300 Hz, air shelf at 12 kHz, optional de-esser at 6 kHz.

4

Render

EQ chain → Multi-band compressor (3 independent bands: low 5:1, mid 3:1, high 2:1) → Hard limiter at −1 dBTP → Loudness normalization to your LUFS target. Output: WAV 24-bit 48 kHz (or configured format) in Audio_Pro/ next to the source folder.
