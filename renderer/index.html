Processing Pipeline

1

Smart Analysis

Runs six parallel measurements: format metadata (FFprobe), integrated loudness (LUFS), noise floor, sibilance level (4–10 kHz energy ratio), reverb estimation (crest factor), and dynamic range. Results automatically configure every subsequent step for this specific file. In Auto mode, the analyzer has full control over all settings.

2

AI Denoise Quality / Auto

DeepFilterNet3 — a neural network trained on thousands of recordings — removes background noise, room reverb, and broadband hum with far better quality than traditional signal processing. Runs on MPS (Apple Silicon GPU) when available, otherwise CPU. In Fast mode, FFmpeg's afftdn filter is used instead. Skipped entirely if the analyzer determines the recording is already clean.

3

Pre-process

Stereo→Mono: Mixes channels to mono (voice is always mono). Noise Gate: Closes signal during silent passages (<0.5% threshold) to eliminate background hum between words. Silence Trim: Removes leading and trailing silence using a double-pass areverse technique to preserve natural mid-speech pauses.

4

BodyGen New in 0.1.2

Harmonic Exciter: Extracts the 80–350 Hz voice body range, then applies analog-style waveshaping (soft saturation) to generate new odd and even harmonics — the same technique used in tube preamps and tape machines. Sub-Harmonic Synthesis: Creates sub-octave content from voice fundamentals (80–200 Hz) via half-wave rectification. The result is a fuller, warmer voice with real new frequency content — not just an EQ boost. Powered by PyTorch for precision DSP.

5

EQ Measure

Applies the full EQ and dynamics chain in measurement mode (no output file) to gather loudness statistics for the two-pass loudnorm algorithm. EQ chain: HPF → warmth shelf (120 Hz) → body boost (180 Hz) → boxiness cut (300 Hz) → clarity (2 kHz) → presence (3.5 kHz) → optional de-esser (6.5 kHz) → harshness tame (8 kHz).

6

Render

EQ chain (HPF → warmth → boxiness cut → clarity → presence) → Parallel compression (100–350 Hz heavily compressed and blended at low volume for consistent body) → Single-band or multi-band compressor → Hard limiter at −1 dBTP → Loudness normalization to your LUFS target. Output: WAV 24-bit 48 kHz in Audio_Pro/ next to the source folder.
