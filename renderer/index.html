Processing Pipeline

1

Analysis ~2s

Detects format, sample rate, bit depth, channels, duration, voice pitch range, and current loudness (LUFS). Results inform every subsequent step automatically.

2

Denoising — DeepFilterNet v3 ~30–60s / 20min

Full-band neural noise suppression at 48kHz. Removes background noise, fans, AC hum, street noise. ARM64 native binary — runs fast on CPU.

3

Speech Enhancement — Resemble Enhance ~3–5min / 20min

AI restoration of speech quality. Removes residual reverb, mic distortion, and compression artifacts. Extends bandwidth and improves clarity. Runs on Apple Silicon GPU (MPS).

4

EQ & Filters ~5s

High-pass filter at 80Hz removes rumble. Adaptive parametric EQ curve auto-selected from pitch analysis. De-esser attenuates sibilants. De-plosive filter cleans transients below 120Hz.

5

Dynamics & Output ~5s

Compressor (3:1 ratio, −20dB threshold) controls dynamic range. Limiter sets ceiling at −1dB. Loudness normalization to −16 LUFS — the podcast/streaming standard. Output: WAV 24-bit 48kHz.
