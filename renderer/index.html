Processing Pipeline

1

Smart Analysis

Runs six parallel measurements: format metadata (FFprobe), integrated loudness (LUFS), noise floor, sibilance level (4–10 kHz energy ratio), reverb estimation (crest factor), and dynamic range. Results automatically configure every subsequent step for this specific file. In Auto mode, the analyzer has full control over all settings.

2

AI Denoise Quality / Auto

DeepFilterNet3 — a neural network trained on thousands of recordings — removes background noise, room reverb, and broadband hum with far better quality than traditional signal processing. Runs on MPS (Apple Silicon GPU) when available, otherwise CPU. In Fast mode, FFmpeg's afftdn filter is used instead. Skipped entirely if the analyzer determines the recording is already clean.

3

Pre-process

Stereo→Mono: Mixes channels to mono (voice is always mono). Noise Gate: Closes signal during silent passages (<0.5% threshold) to eliminate background hum between words. Silence Trim: Removes leading and trailing silence using a double-pass areverse technique to preserve natural mid-speech pauses.

4

EQ Measure

Applies the full EQ and dynamics chain in measurement mode (no output file) to gather loudness statistics for the two-pass loudnorm algorithm. EQ chain: HPF → warmth boost (150 Hz) → mud cut (250 Hz) → body boost (800 Hz) → presence boost (2.5 kHz) → clarity boost (4 kHz) → optional de-esser (6.5 kHz) → air shelf (12 kHz).

5

Render

EQ chain → Multi-band compressor (3 independent bands: low 5:1, mid 3.5:1, high 2:1) → Hard limiter at −1 dBTP → Loudness normalization to your LUFS target using the measured stats from step 4. Output: WAV 24-bit 48 kHz (or configured format) in Audio_Pro/ next to the source folder.
