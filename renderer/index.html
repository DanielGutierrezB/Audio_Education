Processing Pipeline

1

Analysis ~2s

Detects format, sample rate, bit depth, channels, and duration using FFprobe. Results inform every subsequent step automatically — no manual configuration needed.

2

Denoise — afftdn Quality mode only

FFmpeg's adaptive frequency-domain noise filter (afftdn=nf=-25:tn=1). Targets a noise floor of −25 dB with temporal smoothing enabled. Removes background hum, fan noise, and room noise while preserving voice character. Skip with Fast mode for quicker processing.

3

EQ & Filters ~5s

High-pass filter removes rumble (configurable 60–120 Hz). Parametric EQ: presence boost at 2.5 kHz (+2 dB), mud cut at 300 Hz (−2 dB), air shelf at 12 kHz (+1 dB). Optional de-esser at 6 kHz (−3 dB). Two-pass loudness normalization locked to your LUFS target.

4

Dynamics & Output ~5s

3:1 compressor (threshold −20 dBFS) controls dynamic range. Hard limiter at −1 dBTP prevents digital clipping. Final output: WAV 24-bit 48 kHz saved to Audio_Pro/ next to the source folder.
