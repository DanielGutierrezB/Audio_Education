Processing Pipeline

1

Analysis ~2s

Detects format, sample rate, bit depth, channels, and duration using FFprobe. Results inform every subsequent step automatically — no manual configuration needed.

2

Denoise — afftdn Quality mode only

FFmpeg's adaptive frequency-domain noise filter. Targets a noise floor of −25dB with temporal smoothing enabled. Removes background hum, fan noise, and room noise while preserving voice character. Skip with Fast mode.

3

EQ & Filters ~5s

High-pass filter removes rumble (configurable 60–120Hz). Parametric EQ boosts presence at 2.5kHz (+2dB), tames muddiness at 300Hz (−2dB), softens sibilance at 6kHz (−3dB), and adds air at 12kHz (+1dB). Two-pass loudness normalization to your target LUFS.

4

Dynamics & Output ~5s

3:1 compressor (threshold −20dB) controls dynamic range. Hard limiter at −1dBTP prevents clipping. Loudness normalization to target LUFS — the podcast/streaming standard. Output: WAV 24-bit 48kHz saved to Audio_Pro/ next to the source folder.
